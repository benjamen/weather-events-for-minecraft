**Status: draft.** Write the final listing only after the pack has been played in a world (see "Before you list").

**Title:** Weather Events
**One line:** Real weather for Bedrock: puddles, gusting wind, hail, drifting snow, fog and a sun that bites.

## Description
Minecraft's weather is on or off. Weather Events gives it depth. Six kinds of weather, each in light, medium and heavy,
combine into fronts that roll through on a forecast, so a gale builds, brings rain, then clears to mist.

- **Rain with puddles:** thin puddles form on open ground, grow in heavy rain, splash when you walk through, then dry up faster in sun and wind.
- **Wind that pushes:** leaves stream past on the gusts and, in a gale, the wind shoves you downwind. Crouch to brace, shelter behind a wall to stop it.
- **Hail:** pea-sized stones that patter on roofs, marbles that sting, golf balls that hurt and can break glass. A helmet helps.
- **Snow that settles:** flakes drift on the wind and pile up in layers that melt when it stops.
- **Sun and heat:** a strong sun brings a heat haze, hunger, then sunburn if you stay out without a hat or shade.
- **Mist and fog:** still air fills with mist that wind or hot sun burns off.
- **Barometer:** craft one (copper and redstone) to read the weather, what's next and when.

Works alone, or with Deep Night and Natural Disasters (storms use the weather).

## Commands (cheats on)
`/scriptevent wx:set rain 2 wind 3` · `wx:front storm` · `wx:auto` · `wx:climate cold|temperate|hot` · `wx:status`

## Requirements
Minecraft Bedrock 1.26 or newer. Scripting API 2.10. Works in survival, creative and adventure.
